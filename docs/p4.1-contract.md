# P4.1 contract — automatic weekly finalization

**Status: implemented and closed.** This contract supersedes only the mandatory weekly approval lifecycle in the historical P4 contract. All P0–P4 identity, privacy, copied-plan, evidence, scoring, concurrency, audit, and immutable-history boundaries remain authoritative.

## Scope

P4.1 replaces `OPEN -> READY -> APPROVED` with `OPEN -> FINALIZED`. It removes the mandatory responsible approval queue, finalizes a complete week automatically through one explicit operational command, permits finalization after the workspace-local closing boundary even when facts remain missing, and preserves every finalized snapshot when later evidence is amended.

It does not add P5 attention, actions, followups, cases, dashboards, reports, or new product behavior outside weekly finalization.

## Decisions

- A week has only the operational states `OPEN` and `FINALIZED`; legacy `READY` and `APPROVED` rows are migrated to `FINALIZED`.
- Before the workspace-local week closing boundary, finalization requires full applicable evidence coverage.
- After the inclusive week end has passed in the workspace timezone, the week may finalize with missing evidence still visible. The closing boundary is implemented once in PostgreSQL.
- First finalization creates immutable revision 1 and persists `current_revision = 1` on the summary in the same transaction.
- Amending a finalized week requires a reason and the current `row_version`. It recomputes evidence and creates the next immutable revision.
- A stale `row_version` returns `VERSION_CONFLICT` without changing the summary or its history.
- The responsible reviews exceptions and corrections rather than approving every student week.
- Historical approval table names remain internal storage names for migration compatibility; their rows represent immutable finalization revisions after P4.1.
- Legacy `/ready`, `/approve`, and `/corrections` weekly routes are removed. The supported commands are `/finalize` and `/amend`.
